The stack splits into two layers.
1. The **transport layer**: (Bun/Elysia API), `services/rabbitmq` (broker), and `services/smpp` (Rust SMPP
transceiver) accepts message requests over HTTP, persists them, and submits
them to the SMSC, tracking every status transition back to the database.
2. The **product layer** ingests Huawei network alarms from an IMAP
mailbox, resolves who is on call from an escalation ladder synced out of
Microsoft Entra, and pages them by SMS (through the transport layer) and email.
